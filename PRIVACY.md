# Privacy Policy — KstApp

**Effective date:** November 12, 2026
**App:** KstApp (bundle identifier `com.github.KstApp`)
**Developer:** Christian Bayer, WA4YA
**Contact:** chrbayer84@googlemail.com
**Source code:** https://github.com/chrbayer84/KstApp

This Privacy Policy applies to the KstApp iOS application ("the App"). It does
not cover the optional, separately operated push-notification relay server that
some users may choose to run or connect to; the operator of any such server is
responsible for its own privacy practices.

KstApp is an open-source client for the ON4KST amateur-radio chat service. The
developer does not operate a server, does not receive your data, and does not
collect, sell, rent, or share personal information about you.

---

## 1. Data the App Accesses or Collects

The App handles only the data needed to log in to ON4KST, display chat, and
optionally deliver notifications. All of it is entered by you or generated by
your use of the App.

| Data type | Examples | Required? | Where it goes |
|---|---|---|---|
| ON4KST account credentials | Callsign/username, password | Yes, to use chat | ON4KST server; optional user-configured relay |
| Grid square | Maidenhead locator such as `JN79HK` | Optional | ON4KST server; optional relay |
| Chat room selection | e.g. "50/70 MHz", "Microwave" | Yes, to use chat | ON4KST server; optional relay |
| Chat content | Messages you send and receive | Generated by use | ON4KST server |
| Online-user data | Callsigns, grids, and comments of other users | Generated by use | Displayed only |
| Notification preferences | Enabled/disabled, filter (`all` / `myCallsign`) | Optional | Stored on device; optional relay |
| Push token | Apple Push Notification (APNs) device token | Optional (notifications) | Stored on device; optional relay |
| Pushover key | Pushover user key | Optional | Stored on device; optional relay |
| Highlight rules | Sender/message/grid conditions | Optional | Stored on device only |

The App does **not** access or collect:

- Contacts, calendars, photos, camera, or microphone
- Precise or background location (no location services are used)
- Advertising identifiers (IDFA) or other cross-app identifiers
- Health, financial, or biometric data
- Browsing history
- Analytics, usage, or diagnostic telemetry

The App contains **no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting, or tracking
SDKs**, and it does not use Apple's App Tracking Transparency framework because
it does not track you.

## 2. How the Data Is Collected

- **Directly from you:** you type your callsign, password, optional grid square,
  notification preferences, and optional Pushover key into the App.
- **Generated by your use:** chat messages, the list of online users, and the
  room you join are exchanged with the ON4KST service.
- **From iOS:** if you enable notifications, iOS provides an APNs device token.

## 3. How the Data Is Used

- **Authentication:** your callsign and password are sent to ON4KST to log in.
- **Chat functionality:** your grid square, room selection, and messages are
  sent to ON4KST so you can participate in chat.
- **Display:** incoming messages, callsigns, and grids are shown in the App and
  stored in memory for the current session.
- **Notifications (optional):** your notification settings and push token or
  Pushover key are used to alert you about matching messages. If you configure a
  relay server URL, the App sends these settings to that server so it can deliver
  notifications while the App is closed.
- **Local preferences:** highlight rules and settings are stored on your device.

The developer does not use your data for advertising, profiling, analytics, or
any purpose other than providing the App's functionality.

## 4. Third-Party Sharing

The App communicates with the following third parties as part of its normal
operation. The developer does not sell or share data with anyone.

| Recipient | Data | Purpose | Privacy policy |
|---|---|---|---|
| **ON4KST** (`www.on4kst.info`) | Callsign, password, grid square, room, chat messages | Independent amateur-radio chat service | See ON4KST's own policies |
| **Apple Push Notification service (APNs)** | Device token and notification content | Delivering notifications on iOS | https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/ |
| **Optional user-configured relay** | Credentials, grid, room, notification settings, push token/Pushover key | Delivering background notifications, if you choose to configure one | Determined by the relay's operator |
| **Pushover** (only via a relay you configure) | Pushover user key, notification text | Delivering notifications through Pushover, if selected | https://pushover.net/privacy |

You choose whether to enable notifications and whether to configure a relay
server. The App ships with a non-functional placeholder URL; unless you replace it
with a real relay address, no relay server receives your data.

**Important:** the connection to ON4KST uses the service's plain TCP protocol and
is **not encrypted in transit**. Your ON4KST password and chat messages therefore
travel over the network without transport encryption. Do not reuse a sensitive
password for ON4KST.

## 5. Data Storage and Retention

- **On your device:** your callsign, grid square, room, notification settings,
  highlight rules, and push token/Pushover key are stored in the App's settings.
  Your ON4KST password is stored in the **iOS Keychain**, protected by the device
  operating system. Chat messages and the online-user list are kept in memory for
  the current session only.
- **Retention:** device data remains until you change or delete it, or uninstall
  the App. Removing the App generally removes its stored settings and Keychain
  items, subject to iOS behavior.
- **Developer retention:** the developer operates no server and retains no user
  data. Data sent to ON4KST is retained according to ON4KST's practices. Data sent
  to a relay you configure is retained according to that operator's practices.

## 6. Security

- The ON4KST password is stored in the iOS Keychain using
  `kSecAttrAccessibleAfterFirstUnlockThisDeviceOnly`, so it is not included in
  device backups or migrated to another device.
- Non-secret preferences are stored in the App's standard preferences.
- As noted above, the ON4KST chat protocol is unencrypted; data in transit to
  ON4KST is not protected by TLS.
- If you configure a relay, use an `https://` URL to protect credentials in
  transit.

## 7. Your Rights and Choices

Because the developer holds no data about you, there is nothing for the developer
to export, correct, or delete. You can control your data directly:

- **Withdraw consent / stop data flows:** disable notifications, clear the relay
  URL, or disconnect/stop using the App.
- **Delete data on your device:** change or clear your credentials in the App, or
  uninstall the App. This removes locally stored settings and the Keychain
  password.
- **Delete data held by ON4KST:** contact ON4KST directly.
- **Delete data held by a relay:** contact the operator of the relay you
  configured, or use that server's deletion endpoint if provided.
- **Questions or requests:** contact the developer at
  **chrbayer84@googlemail.com**. If you are in the EEA/UK, you may also lodge a
  complaint with your local data-protection authority.

## 8. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or the applicable age of digital
consent in your region). The developer does not knowingly collect data from
children because the developer collects no data at all.

## 9. International Data Transfers

The developer does not transfer your data internationally. ON4KST, Apple, and any
relay you configure may process data in other countries according to their own
policies.
